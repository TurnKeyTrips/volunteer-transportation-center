The Volunteer Transportation Center is seeking a full-time **Transportation Coordinator** to join our Watertown team. This is a rewarding, people-focused role at the heart of our mission: matching volunteer drivers with the neighbors who count on them to reach medical, social, and essential appointments.

## What you'll do

- Schedule and confirm daily rides, pairing clients with available volunteer drivers.
- Serve as a friendly first point of contact for clients calling our ride lines.
- Keep accurate records of trips, mileage, and driver availability.
- Support volunteer drivers with routes, questions, and day-of changes.

## What we're looking for

- Strong organizational skills and a calm, helpful phone manner.
- Comfort with scheduling software and basic data entry.
- A genuine care for the people we serve across Northern New York.
- A valid driver's license and reliable transportation.

## How to apply

Download the application above, complete it, and email it to our Human Resources team using the contact information below. We review applications as they arrive and will reach out to set up a conversation.
